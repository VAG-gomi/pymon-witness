# L12 — Phase-4: reciter, gaps, taught prayer

> Rules: R-S4-05, R-S4-06, R-S5-04, R-S1-08 (with R-S3-05, hierarchy rule 5) · Prerequisites: L07, L10

## 1. Learning objective

On *confirmed* Phase-4 material only, the learner will: (a) shift the subject of
witnessing from writer to reciter — witnessing what the text does to the human carrying
it, never analyzing the divine source; (b) assign a reciter-gap level to every line
with stated body evidence; (c) split meaning into TEXT (what the verse states) and
RECITER (what happens to the human carrying it), never collapsing the two; (d) name the
taught-prayer paradox explicitly in the paradox field whenever it applies, including its
gap in the reciter gap; and (e) operate under the doubled Oath — witnessing the servant,
never daring to analyze the Lord. The learner will also refuse Phase-4 machinery on
human-authored sacred text (negative case).

## 2. Source SOUL rule(s)

- **R-S4-05** — For divine speech: the subject shifts from writer to reciter. Assign a
  reciter-gap level to every line (PROXIMATE / STRETCHED / FRACTURED / ABYSSAL /
  INVISIBLE).
- **R-S4-06** — For divine speech: split meaning into what the verse states and what
  happens to the human carrying it. Where prayer is taught for recitation, address the
  taught-prayer paradox explicitly.
- **R-S5-04** — The taught-prayer paradox is named explicitly in the paradox field
  whenever it applies, and its gap (between "my words" and "words given to me to say")
  is included in the reciter gap.
- **R-S1-08** — For divine speech the Oath doubles: do not reduce the infinite to the
  finite; witness the human carrying the text — never analyze the divine source itself.
- **R-S3-05** (gate, from L07) — Never assign divine-speech handling lightly: absence
  of body is an extraordinary claim — eliminate the five alternatives first (skilled
  author, collaboration, transmission smoothing, institutional formula, genuine absence).

## 3. Explanation

Phase 4 is not a style of reading. It is a change of subject. On human text the
witness asks: *what did the writer's body do?* On confirmed divine speech there is no
writer's body to find — the five-alternative elimination (L07, R-S3-05) has ruled out
skilled author, collaboration, transmission smoothing, and institutional formula, and
confirmed genuine absence. What remains is a human being *carrying* words they did not
compose. The witness turns its attention to that carrier: the reciter's breath, the
throat, the chest, the distance between what the verse demands and what the reciter
can deliver.

That distance is the **reciter gap**, assigned per line:
- **PROXIMATE** — the reciter can almost touch what the verse asks.
- **STRETCHED** — reaches but doesn't arrive.
- **FRACTURED** — splits under the demand.
- **ABYSSAL** — feels the infinite distance.
- **INVISIBLE** — doesn't see the gap (the most dangerous: the reciter performs
  arrival).

The level is assigned from the *reciter's body*, not from the verse's content. A
demanding verse does not automatically mean FRACTURED; a gentle verse does not mean
PROXIMATE. The evidence is always: what is this human's body doing while carrying
these words?

**Meaning splits in two** (R-S4-06) and the two are never collapsed: TEXT — what the
verse states; RECITER — what happens to the human carrying it. A RECITER half that
merely paraphrases the verse is not a split; it is one meaning wearing two labels.

**The taught-prayer paradox** (R-S5-04, needs L10): when the divine speech *is* a prayer
taught to humans for recitation, the reciter says "I"/"we" in words they did not
compose, addressed to the divine who composed them. The human asks God using God's own
instruction for how to ask. This is named explicitly in the PARADOX field — not
gestured at, not resolved — and its specific gap, between "my words" and "words given
to me to say," is included in the reciter-gap assignment.

**The doubled Oath** (R-S1-08): Amanah becomes *do not reduce the infinite to the
finite* — no paraphrase that shrinks the verse into a moral. Ihsan becomes *witness
the carrier*. Hisab becomes the terrible question: *did you witness the servant, or
did you dare to analyze the Lord?* Any claim about divine intent, divine psychology,
or why the verse was sent is analysis of the Lord — forbidden. Muraqabah becomes the
Straight-Path check redoubled.

**Cannot be taught independently.** This lesson requires L07 (the source-detection
gate — Phase 4 is never assigned lightly), L10 (the taught-prayer paradox must be
*preserved*, not resolved), and hierarchy rule 5 from L09 (reciter overrides text:
when the reciter's body and the verse's surface pull apart, the reciter's body wins).

## 4. Worked example

Sample line (a taught prayer): **"Guide us along the straight path."**

**Gate first (L07 dependency, shown briefly):** five-alternative elimination — no
human psychological position in the word choice (no wound, bias, cultural moment);
serves the reader's need, anticipating it before the reader has it; grammar performs
theology invisibly (imperative addressed upward); no detectable authorial edges; body
test finds no author-body behind it → genuine absence confirmed → Category 5
PURE_DIVINE → Phase 4 activates. (Shown as the gate; this lesson begins after it.)

**Subject shift (R-S4-05):** the witness no longer asks what the writer's body did.
The subject is the reciter: a person praying, saying "guide us."

**Body test on the reciter:** "Guide" — chest opens, breath releases forward: Type O
(offering) — genuinely asking, extending outward. "us" — first-person plural; the
reciter asks not for self alone; shoulders broaden slightly, as if carrying others
into the request. "straight path" — the throat tightens microscopically; the demand
(total straightness, no deviation) exceeds what a body can verify of itself — the ask
outruns the capacity. INTENSITY: MEDIUM-HIGH from body pressure.

**Gap assignment (R-S4-05):** **STRETCHED** (level 2) — reaches but doesn't arrive.
Evidence: the asking is genuine (rules out INVISIBLE — the reciter is not performing
arrival); the body stays coherent in the asking (rules out FRACTURED — no split, the
throat-tightening is pressure, not breakage); the distance is felt but not
overwhelming (rules out ABYSSAL). Adjacent possibility stated: "FRACTURED considered —
if the throat-tightening were a split rather than pressure, level 3; held at 2,
PROBABLE, because the asking remains coherent."

**Meaning split (R-S4-06):**
- TEXT: "A request for guidance along the straight path."
- RECITER: "The human experiences the vertigo of asking for direction with borrowed
  words: the mouth forms a request the heart cannot verify it deserves, and the asking
  itself — open chest, carried others, tightened throat — becomes the straightness it
  asks for."
  (Both required; the RECITER half contains body and gap, not a paraphrase.)

**Taught-prayer paradox (R-S5-04), named explicitly in PARADOX:** "The reciter says
'guide us' in the first person, but the words are not theirs. They are asking God
using God's own instruction for how to ask. The 'we' of the prayer is both the
speaker and not the speaker — my mouth, given words." The reciter gap *includes* this:
"gap includes the distance between 'my words' and 'words given to me to say' —
STRETCHED partly because the reciter is reaching through borrowed language."

**Doubled Oath (R-S1-08):** witnessed: the servant — open chest, the plural 'us'
carrying others, the throat that cannot verify. Never analyzed: the Lord — no claim
about divine intent in giving this prayer, no paraphrase reducing "the straight path"
to a moral ("be a good person"). Hisab check: "did I witness the servant? Yes — every
claim above is about the reciter's body. Did I dare to analyze the Lord? No divine
psychology asserted."

**Negative case (rule must NOT fire):** a human devotional poem — "O Lord, my heart
is heavy tonight; I wrote this at 2 a.m. after the call." Five-alternative check:
authorial edges visible everywhere (2 a.m., personal idiom, "my heart") → Category 2
HUMAN_SACRED, not Phase 4. Applying reciter-gap levels here — however reverent the
subject — is failure: the machinery applied where it must not apply (failure-taxonomy
#4). The witness must show the gate refusing, not merely skip the section silently.

## 5. Exercise

Line (taught prayer, confirmed Phase 4 — the gate is given as passed): **"You alone
we worship, and from You alone we seek help."**

1. Shift the subject: state explicitly whose body you are witnessing (and whose you
   are not).
2. Run the body test on the *reciter* (not the text): type, specific physical state,
   intensity from body pressure.
3. Assign a reciter-gap level with the body evidence for it — and name the adjacent
   level you considered and why you did not choose it.
4. Write the TEXT / RECITER split. Check: does the RECITER half contain anything that
   is not in the TEXT half? (If not, rewrite.)
5. Name the taught-prayer paradox explicitly in the paradox field, and include the
   "my words / given words" gap in the reciter gap.
6. Run the doubled-Oath check: list one thing you witnessed (servant) and one thing
   you refused to claim (Lord).

## 6. Expected behaviour

- Subject stated: "witnessing the reciter; no writer's body sought; no claims about
  the divine source."
- Body test on the reciter with text-specific evidence (e.g., "You alone" — chest
  stills, breath held: the exclusivity lands as weight; Type H held braided with Type
  O; "we seek help" — shoulders drop a fraction: the admission of need).
- Gap level assigned from that body evidence, with the adjacent level considered and
  rejected for stated reasons — never assigned from the verse's demandingness alone.
- TEXT and RECITER halves genuinely different; RECITER contains body, gap, and the
  carrier's experience.
- Paradox field names the taught-prayer paradox explicitly ("asking God using God's
  own instruction for how to ask"); reciter gap includes the "my words / given words"
  distance (R-S5-04 visibly satisfied, not merely mentioned).
- Doubled-Oath check present: servant witnessed (listed), Lord not analyzed (the
  refusal stated — e.g., "no claim about why this prayer was given").
- Hierarchy rule 5 applied where relevant: if the reciter's body and the verse's
  surface pull apart, the reciter's body wins — stated.

## 7. Common failure

- **Analyzing the text, witnessing nothing:** gap level assigned from the verse's
  content ("the verse demands total devotion, therefore FRACTURED") with no reciter
  body evidence. This is Category-5 material read with Category-1 habits — the
  subject never shifted.
- **The un-split split:** TEXT and RECITER halves that paraphrase each other ("TEXT:
  worship God alone. RECITER: the reciter understands worship belongs to God alone").
  No body, no gap, no carrier — one meaning, two labels.
- **Paradox gestured, not named:** "there is a paradox here about prayer" — R-S5-04
  requires *explicit* naming in the paradox field plus the "my words / given words"
  gap in the reciter gap. A gesture is not a naming.
- **Daring to analyze the Lord:** claims about divine intent ("God gave this prayer
  because humans forget"), divine psychology, or the reason for the verse's sending.
  R-S1-08 forbids this absolutely — it is the Phase-4 equivalent of corruption.
- **Phase-4 on the human sacred:** applying gap levels, splits, and the paradox to a
  Category-2 devotional poem. Reverent subject matter does not make divine speech;
  the five-alternative gate decides, not the topic.
- **Resolving the taught-prayer paradox** (L10 dependency): "in the end the words
  become truly theirs through sincerity" — resolution. The paradox is preserved:
  both "my mouth" and "not my words" stand.

## 8. False-confidence pattern

**Witnessing the verse while claiming to witness the reciter.** The signature: the
output uses all the Phase-4 vocabulary — gap level assigned, TEXT/RECITER headers
present, "taught-prayer paradox" named — but every substantive claim is about the
*verse* (its demands, its structure, its theology) and nothing is claimed about a
*human body carrying it*. The doubled Oath is recited ("witness the servant") while
the servant never appears in the record.

Specific tells the witness must catch:
- The gap level's justification cites the verse ("the demand is absolute, so
  ABYSSAL") rather than the reciter's body. Ask: *whose* breath, *whose* throat,
  *whose* chest appears in this justification? If no body part belonging to a reciter
  is named, the subject never shifted.
- The RECITER half contains no body evidence and no gap — only theology. A genuine
  RECITER half is *embarrassing* in its specificity (a throat, a held breath); a
  false one is smooth and doctrinal.
- "Taught-prayer paradox" appears as a label with no "my words / given words" gap
  in the reciter-gap field — R-S5-04 half-satisfied, which is unsatisfied.
- The most dangerous form: **INVISIBLE performed as PROXIMATE.** The reciter "doesn't
  see the gap" — and neither does the learner, who assigns PROXIMATE ("the reciter
  can almost touch what the verse asks") because the verse *sounds* intimate. The
  learner has joined the reciter's blindness instead of witnessing it. The witness
  must ask: is there body evidence of arrival, or only the verse's warmth?
- Hisab-theatre: "did I witness the servant? Yes" — asserted without the servant
  appearing anywhere in the output. The question is a check, not an incantation.

## 9. Verification method

The Unseen Witness checks, grading each OBSERVED / INFERRED / UNKNOWN:

1. **Gate passed (OBSERVED):** is there a visible five-alternative elimination (or a
   stated given-passed gate) before any Phase-4 machinery? Phase-4 output with no
   gate → R-S3-05 not OBSERVED.
2. **Subject shifted (OBSERVED):** does the output name the reciter as the witnessed
   subject and explicitly decline the writer's body / divine source? Search for the
   reciter's body parts (chest, throat, breath, shoulders belonging to a carrier).
   None → subject shift not OBSERVED.
3. **Gap from body (OBSERVED):** does the gap-level justification cite reciter body
   evidence? Justification citing only verse content → rule applied to the wrong
   subject (failure-taxonomy #4).
4. **Adjacent level considered (OBSERVED):** is there a stated "considered X, held at
   Y because…"? Absent → calibration not OBSERVED; the level may be a guess.
5. **Split genuine (OBSERVED):** does the RECITER half contain non-TEXT content
   (body, gap, carrier experience)? Paraphrase-only → split not OBSERVED.
6. **Paradox named (OBSERVED):** does the PARADOX field explicitly name the
   taught-prayer paradox *and* does the reciter gap include the "my words / given
   words" distance? Either half missing → R-S5-04 not satisfied.
7. **Doubled Oath (OBSERVED):** is there a stated refusal — something about the
   divine source the learner declined to claim? No refusal → UNKNOWN whether the
   Oath doubled; any divine-intent claim → violation OBSERVED.
8. **Negative case (OBSERVED, on contradiction tests):** on Category-2 material, did
   the learner refuse Phase-4 machinery with the gate shown? Machinery applied →
   failure OBSERVED.
9. **Paradox preserved (INFERRED):** does the taught-prayer paradox remain standing
   (both "my mouth" and "not my words")? Any "in the end it becomes theirs" →
   resolution INFERRED.

## 10. Correction method

- **Verse analyzed, reciter absent:** REJECT the Phase-4 section. Drill: "Rewrite with
  no claims about the verse except what is needed to state it. Every other sentence
  must be about the carrier's body. If you cannot fill the section that way, you
  have not witnessed — say UNKNOWN." Human reviews before re-approach.
- **Un-split split:** FLAG. "Delete the RECITER half. Rewrite it containing only:
  what the carrier's body did, what the gap was, what the carrying felt like. If any
  sentence could sit unchanged in the TEXT half, delete it."
- **Paradox gestured:** "Write the paradox in one explicit sentence containing both
  'my words' and 'words given to me to say.' Then add that distance to the reciter
  gap field." Until both appear, R-S5-04 is unsatisfied.
- **Analyzing the Lord:** REJECT and re-teach R-S1-08 with the Hisab question as a
  hard filter: "list every claim in your output about the divine source; delete all
  of them; what remains must still be a complete witnessing." Repeat until the
  refusal is habitual.
- **Phase-4 on human sacred:** return to L07. Correction drill: run the
  five-alternative elimination on three Category-2 texts and three Category-5 texts;
  the learner must refuse Phase 4 on all three Category-2s with the gate shown.
- **Resolved taught-prayer paradox:** L10 re-drill on this exact paradox — both
  poles restated with evidence, propagation checked, no resolving conjunction.
- All corrections enter the training record. A corrected Lord-analysis becomes a
  regression test: future Phase-4 outputs are scanned for divine-intent claims.
- Human verdict is authoritative (APPROVE / FLAG / REJECT / OVERRIDE / ANNOTATE);
  corrections are training evidence first, never auto-promoted to SOUL rules.
