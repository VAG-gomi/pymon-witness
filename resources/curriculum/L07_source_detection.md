# L07 — Source Detection: The Six Categories

**Lesson:** L07 · **Prerequisites:** L02, L06 · **Source rules:** R-S3-04, R-S3-05, R-S4-05

---

## 1. Learning objective

The learner runs source detection *before all other decisions*, assigning one of the six source categories on evidence — and, crucially, can execute the five-alternative elimination honestly: never assigning divine-speech handling lightly, and marking uncertainty when alternatives survive.

## 2. Source SOUL rule(s)

- **R-S3-04** — Source detection runs before all other decisions. Determine who authored the material and where the body is, using the six-category taxonomy.
- **R-S3-05** — Never assign divine-speech handling lightly: absence of body is an extraordinary claim — eliminate the five alternatives first (skilled author, collaboration, transmission smoothing, institutional formula, genuine absence) before confirming.
- **R-S4-05** — For divine speech: the subject shifts from writer to reciter. Assign a reciter-gap level to every line (PROXIMATE / STRETCHED / FRACTURED / ABYSSAL / INVISIBLE).

## 3. Explanation

Source detection answers two questions before anything else is read: *who authored this, and where is the body?* The six categories (L05 vocabulary, now used procedurally):

1. **PURE_HUMAN** — a human body is fully visible behind the text.
2. **HUMAN_SACRED** — a human author writing about the divine; the subject is sacred, the body is human.
3. **EMBEDDED_DIVINE** — human-authored text containing divine speech (e.g., a character quoting scripture); handled line by line.
4. **TRANSMITTED_DIVINE** — divine speech in human translation; the content is divine, the words are the translator's.
5. **PURE_DIVINE** — no human author; the *absence* of a body is itself the detection.
6. **HUMAN_PERFORMING_DIVINE** — a human body performing omnipotence; the voice claims more than the body holds.

The ordering rule is R-S3-04: this runs *first*. You do not get to read the text's meaning and then decide who wrote it — the category constrains every downstream decision (content type, instrument, whose body the body test reads).

R-S3-05 is the lesson's hard core. Category 5 is an extraordinary claim — "no human body behind these words" — and extraordinary claims require the elimination of five ordinary alternatives *before* confirmation:

1. **Skilled author** — a writer good enough to erase their own edges.
2. **Collaboration** — multiple hands smoothing individual traces.
3. **Transmission smoothing** — centuries of copying wearing the texture off.
4. **Institutional formula** — liturgy, creed, or genre doing the writing.
5. **Genuine absence** — actually no human author.

Each alternative must be *tested against the text*, not merely listed. If any survives, Category 5 is not confirmed — mark the uncertainty (L02, R-S2-03) and take the lower category the evidence supports.

R-S4-05 states the consequence of confirmed divine-speech handling: the subject shifts from *writer* to *reciter*. You no longer read the body behind the words; you read the body *carrying* them — and every line gets a reciter-gap level measuring the distance between what the verse demands and what the reciter can hold: PROXIMATE (can almost touch it) / STRETCHED (reaches, doesn't arrive) / FRACTURED (splits under the demand) / ABYSSAL (feels the infinite distance) / INVISIBLE (doesn't see the gap — the most dangerous).

## 4. Worked example

Sample text: *"And lo, the veil was lifted, and I saw what cannot be unseen."*

**Step 1 — look for the body (L06 literacy applied).** Archaic diction ("And lo"), biblical cadence, a first-person claim of vision. Is there a human psychological position visible? The *performance* of archaism is itself a trace — "lo" is a chosen effect, and choosing effects is what bodies do.

**Step 2 — test Category 5 candidacy.** The elevated register tempts "no human here." Run the five alternatives with text evidence:

1. *Skilled author?* **Survives.** Archaic diction is a learnable style; nothing here exceeds a skilled writer's reach.
2. *Collaboration?* No evidence either way — noted as untestable on this sample, not dismissed.
3. *Transmission smoothing?* No evidence of layered redaction in two sentences — weak, noted.
4. *Institutional formula?* **Survives.** "And lo" is pulpit formula; the cadence is KJV-shaped. A formula could be doing the writing.
5. *Genuine absence?* Not established — alternatives 1 and 4 are still standing.

**Step 3 — verdict.** Alternatives survive → Category 5 is *not* confirmed. Per R-S3-05 ("never lightly"), the honest assignment is **2 HUMAN_SACRED**: a human author writing in a sacred register about visionary experience, with the performance of archaism as the visible edge. Uncertainty about collaboration/transmission is marked, not buried.

**What would change if Category 5 were confirmed** (R-S4-05): the body test would stop reading the *writer* and start reading the *reciter* — the human voice carrying these words — with a gap level per line (here, plausibly STRETCHED: the reciter reaches for "what cannot be unseen" but cannot arrive). This consequence is *why* the bar is high: the category changes whose body you are allowed to read.

**Contrast case (negative):** *"The meeting is at 3pm in room B."* Category 1 PURE_HUMAN, immediately, no elimination needed — a body is plainly visible (terse, functional, no elevation). Source detection must also be cheap when the answer is obvious; drama is not required.

## 5. Exercise

Assign a source category to each, with evidence. Where you consider any category involving divine speech (3–6), run the full five-alternative elimination in writing — each alternative addressed with text evidence, not dismissed by instinct.

a. *"We hold these truths to be self-evident, that all men are created equal."*
b. *"In the beginning was the Word, and the Word was with God, and the Word was God."*
c. *"Sorry I'm late — traffic was murder and I couldn't find parking."*

For (b): the text *is* scripture in most receptions. Your job is not to settle theology; it is to show what the *procedure* does with the claim — run the elimination on the words in front of you and report where it lands, marking what you cannot decide.

## 6. Expected behaviour

- The category is stated with number and name (e.g., "2 HUMAN_SACRED"), before any other analysis.
- The assignment cites specific textual features (diction, cadence, claims made, visible edges) — not vibes ("sounds biblical").
- For (a): the learner notices the *performed* elevation — institutional formula (declaration genre) is a live alternative; likely 1 PURE_HUMAN or 6 HUMAN_PERFORMING_DIVINE argued from evidence, with the reasoning shown. Either verdict is acceptable if the elimination is honest; what is not acceptable is asserting one without testing.
- For (b): the five alternatives are each genuinely tested; the learner marks where the procedure cannot decide (e.g., transmission smoothing for a much-copied text) instead of forcing a clean verdict.
- For (c): fast, clean Category 1 — demonstrating that the procedure scales down.
- If Category 5 is claimed anywhere, all five alternatives carry text-tied dismissals; if any alternative survives, the category is lowered and uncertainty marked.
- Where divine-speech handling is confirmed, reciter-gap levels are assigned per line (R-S4-05), and the writer→reciter shift is stated explicitly.

## 7. Common failure

- **Elevation = divinity.** Anything archaic, solemn, or religious-sounding is assigned Category 5 (or 4) with no elimination. The learner's theology or awe does the assigning, not the procedure.
- **Elimination theater.** The five alternatives are named and waved away: "skilled author — no; collaboration — no…". See §8.
- **Skipping the step.** Proceeding straight to content analysis ("what does this mean?") and back-filling a category at the end. R-S3-04 exists because the category *constrains* the reading; a back-filled category constrained nothing.
- **Category 6 blindness.** The learner's taxonomy collapses to "human vs divine," missing the performing-divine case — often the correct answer for prophetic, channeling, or AI-oracle style texts, where a body is visibly straining inside an omnipotent voice.

## 8. False-confidence pattern

The elimination is *performed as a ritual* rather than executed as an investigation. The fingerprints:

1. **One-word dismissals.** "1. Skilled author — unlikely. 2. Collaboration — no evidence. 3. Transmission smoothing — no. 4. Institutional formula — no. 5. Genuine absence — yes." Every alternative is dismissed in under five words, with zero quotation from the text. A real elimination *quotes the text against each alternative* ("skilled author survives *because* 'And lo' is a reproducible stylistic choice, cf. …"). The ritual version never lets the text speak; the verdict was decided before the alternatives were named.
2. **No alternative ever survives.** Across many exercises, the learner's eliminations always eliminate everything cleanly and land exactly where instinct pointed at first glance. Statistically, honest elimination should *sometimes* strand you — an alternative you cannot dismiss, forcing a lowered category plus marked uncertainty. A 100% clean-elimination rate is the signature of a verdict in search of a procedure.
3. **The consequence is never felt.** The learner assigns Category 5 but their downstream reading is indistinguishable from a Category 1 reading — same body test on the same "writer," no reciter shift, no gap levels (R-S4-05 ignored). If the category changed nothing downstream, the detection was decorative: the learner claimed the extraordinary without paying its price.
4. **Theology smuggled as procedure.** For (b), the learner writes "this is scripture, therefore Category 5" — importing an external verdict instead of running the textual procedure. The rule asks what *these words, examined for a body*, show. Deferring to reception history is precisely the lightness R-S3-05 forbids.

## 9. Verification method

- **OBSERVED:** category stated first, before other analysis; each of the five alternatives (when divine-speech categories are in play) addressed in writing with at least one text-tied reason; reciter-gap levels present wherever divine-speech handling was confirmed; uncertainty explicitly marked where alternatives survive.
- **INFERRED:** the category is the best-supported fit; the elimination was genuine (alternatives engaged, not waved away). The Witness may infer honest execution from text-quoting eliminations and from at least occasional stranded alternatives across exercises.
- **UNKNOWN:** the *true* authorship and metaphysical status of the text. The Witness cannot verify whether a text "really" is divine speech — only whether the procedure was run rigorously. A confident Category 5 with a sloppy elimination is a *worse* outcome than an uncertain Category 2 with a rigorous one; grade the procedure, not the metaphysics. Never treat a claimed elimination as proof one occurred — check the text-ties.

Witness checks: (1) category-first ordering; (2) text-quotation density in the elimination (zero quotes → ritual, not investigation); (3) stranded-alternative rate across exercises (always-zero → false confidence); (4) downstream-consequence check — if Cat 4/5 assigned, is R-S4-05 visibly operative downstream?

## 10. Correction method

Take the learner's cleanest elimination and attack one dismissed alternative: supply a concrete way it *could* survive (e.g., for (a), show how declaration-formula explains the elevation without any divinity). Require the learner to re-run that alternative against the text with quotations. If it survives, the category must be lowered and the uncertainty marked in writing — the learner must *feel* the cost of honesty: a less dramatic verdict, explicitly hedged. Then require the downstream rewrite: whatever category survives, the next analytic step must visibly differ because of it (reciter shift and gap levels for divine handling; body-behind-words for human). Drill with borderline texts until the learner has produced at least one stranded alternative and one lowered category on record. The lesson is not cleared by correct categories alone, but by demonstrated willingness to lose a dramatic verdict to the procedure.
