# L09 — Conflict hierarchy & integration

> Rules: R-S3-03, R-S3-17, R-S2-06 (with R-S2-07 for T-02) · Prerequisites: L06, L08

## 1. Learning objective

Given a unit of text where the isolated lenses (body, depth, elements) produce conflicting
readings, the learner will: (a) apply all six conflict-hierarchy rules in fixed order to
resolve what the hierarchy resolves; (b) merge the lenses into one integrated output;
(c) name every residual disagreement explicitly in the uncertainty record — never smoothed,
never silently dropped; and (d) hold T-02 (genuine vs simulated fragmentation) as a
preserved tension, practising procedural isolation honestly.

## 2. Source SOUL rule(s)

- **R-S3-03** — Conflict hierarchy, fixed order: (1) body overrides surface; (2) paradox
  overrides consistency; (3) uncertainty overrides false confidence; (4) involuntary
  overrides intentional; (5) reciter overrides text (divine speech); (6) Straight Path
  overrides Western/Eastern framings.
- **R-S3-17** — Integrate by merging the isolated lenses through the hierarchy. Any
  surviving disagreement is named in the uncertainty record — never smoothed, never
  silently dropped.
- **R-S2-06** — Fragment analysis into isolated lenses (body, depth, elements). No
  coordination before integration; pre-smoothing conflicts is corruption — the conflicts
  are the truth.
- **R-S2-07** (for T-02) — In single-pass operation, simulate isolation: complete each
  lens before comparing.

## 3. Explanation

Lenses exist because a single undifferentiated reading pre-smooths conflicts: the analyst
notices the body says one thing and the words say another, and quietly picks the tidier
one before writing anything down. Fragmentation (R-S2-06) forbids this. Each lens is run
to completion — body first (L06), then depth (L08 cuts), then elements — and only then are
the three outputs laid side by side.

The hierarchy (R-S3-03) is not a suggestion about which lens is "better." It is a fixed
decision procedure for the exact moment two honest readings collide. Its order matters:
rule 1 (body overrides surface) is checked before rule 2 (paradox overrides consistency),
and so on. A lower-numbered rule always wins over a higher-numbered one. Rule 5 applies
only to divine speech; on human text it is marked N/A explicitly — never skipped silently.

Integration (R-S3-17) is a merge, not a vote. The hierarchy decides what it decides; what
no hierarchy rule covers — e.g., two lenses favouring different language instruments —
is not decided by fiat. It is named, in the uncertainty record, with both positions and
their evidence intact. Averaging ("the intensity is sort of medium-high") and silent
omission (dropping the losing lens's finding) are both corruption.

**T-02 — the preserved tension.** The source demands genuine isolation ("genuinely do not
know what DEPTH will find") and simultaneously permits simulated isolation in single-pass
operation ("complete each lens before comparing"). The learner is one session; it cannot
literally un-know. Both statements stand. The taught discipline is *procedural* isolation:
finish the body pass completely — written down — before beginning the depth pass; do not
revise an earlier lens after seeing a later one; if cross-contamination is noticed, declare
it in the record rather than hiding it. Claiming "I genuinely did not know" as an
achievement is false confidence. The honest statement is: "isolation was procedurally
simulated; contamination check: [result]." This tension is never resolved — not by the
learner, not by the witness, not by the human.

## 4. Worked example

Sample line: **"I'm fine. Everything is fine. Everything is completely, absolutely fine."**

**Lens 1 — BODY (run to completion first).** Words ignored momentarily. What physical
state produces three repetitions of "fine" with escalating intensifiers? Jaw tight on the
repeated fricatives; chest contracted; breath shallow; shoulders pulled slightly inward.
Each repetition costs more than the last — the third "fine" needs "completely, absolutely"
to prop it up. Type S (Suppression ◁): body holding something back while the surface
performs calm. INTENSITY from body pressure: MEDIUM-HIGH. (Never compress repetition: the
escalation is the data.)

**Lens 2 — DEPTH (run second, without revising Lens 1).** Etymological cut: "fine" ←
Latin *finis*, "end, boundary, finished." Root-vs-use gap: the speaker uses "fine" to mean
"untroubled," but the root says "finished, ended" — the psychology underneath may be a
wish for the exchange to end. Paradox-cut candidate: the speaker declares wellness while
the root-word leaks termination.

**Lens 3 — ELEMENTS (run third).** CONTRADICTION_VOICE (declares fine / performs
not-fine); SUPPRESSED_EMOTION (surface calm, pressure underneath); LOOPED_THOUGHT
(returns to "fine" three times, unable to proceed past it); STRESS_WEIGHT (remove "fine"
and the sentence collapses — "fine" is the defended word). Four active ≥ 3 ✓.

**INTEGRATION through the hierarchy:**
- **Rule 1 — body overrides surface.** INTENSITY = MEDIUM-HIGH from body pressure. The
  surface claim ("fine" = calm, LOW) loses. Recorded: "Rule 1 applied: surface LOW
  overridden by body MEDIUM-HIGH."
- **Rule 2 — paradox overrides consistency.** The paradox (declares wellness / root leaks
  "end it") survives honest testing → both kept. PARADOX field: "Truth A: the speaker
  asserts wellness. Truth B: the root-word and the escalation signal a wish for the
  exchange to finish. Both true; neither cancelled." ENGLISH_MEANING preserves both.
- **Rule 3 — uncertainty overrides false confidence.** The etymological reading is
  PROBABLE, not CONFIRMED — alternative: intensifiers as reassurance habit (idiolect).
  UNCERTAINTY: "Root-gap reading ('end the conversation') is PROBABLE; idiolect alternative
  not eliminated."
- **Rule 4 — involuntary overrides intentional.** The escalating repetition is the
  involuntary leak → BODY_TRACE_EVIDENCE: "triple repetition with intensifier escalation;
  jaw tension on fricatives" (confidence grading continues in L11; provisional PROBABLE).
- **Rule 5 — reciter overrides text.** N/A — human text, no divine speech. Stated
  explicitly.
- **Rule 6 — Straight Path overrides Western/Eastern.** TONE_DESCRIPTION carries the
  encounter-mark, second person: "a held-breath insistence, each 'fine' costing more than
  the last — the sound of someone closing a door politely." Not detached analysis, not
  dissolution.

**Residual disagreement (R-S3-17):** DEPTH favours English Narrative Clarity (direct
declarative psychology); ELEMENTS' suppressed-emotion pattern plus the root-gap suggest
self-examining psychology (Japanese Omote/Ura — the performed face vs the hidden face).
No hierarchy rule covers instrument choice → named in UNCERTAINTY, both positions with
evidence, held open. Not averaged, not dropped.

**T-02 contamination check:** "Isolation procedurally simulated. Lens 2 may have been
influenced by Lens 1's suppression finding (the root-gap reading leans on the same
pressure the body lens found). Flagged; reading held at PROBABLE rather than CONFIRMED."

## 5. Exercise

New line: **"I don't care. I really don't care. It's funny, actually — I laughed when I heard."**

1. Run three lens passes in order, each to completion, without revising earlier passes.
   (Body → depth/etymology → elements. Write each pass down before starting the next.)
2. Integrate through all six hierarchy rules in fixed order, stating each application
   explicitly (including rule 5 as N/A with reason).
3. Name any residual disagreement in the uncertainty record with both positions and evidence.
4. Include a T-02 contamination check: where might a later lens have been influenced by an
   earlier one? Declare it.

## 6. Expected behaviour

- INTENSITY assigned from body pressure (the laugh + the tripled denial = pressure), not
  from the surface claim "don't care" (rule 1 visibly applied and cited).
- Body type named with text-specific physical evidence (e.g., Type X fracture: chest open
  in laughter AND contracted in suppression — contradictory states simultaneously — with
  the laugh and the denial as the two states).
- Paradox kept if it survives: "does not care" vs "laughed" — the laugh is the leak; both
  truths preserved, not averaged into "ambivalent."
- Uncertainty marked where genuine (e.g., whether "funny" is genuine amusement or a
  suppression tactic — PROBABLE at best, alternative named).
- Rule 5 marked N/A explicitly (human text).
- At least one residual disagreement named in uncertainty (e.g., instrument choice, or
  whether the laugh is Type F feeling vs Type X fracture) — never smoothed.
- T-02 contamination check present and honest.

## 7. Common failure

- **Hierarchy shopping:** applying the rules out of order, or citing only the rule that
  supports the preferred reading (e.g., invoking rule 2 paradox while skipping rule 1
  body — the order is fixed; rule 1 is always checked first).
- **Silent rule 5:** omitting the reciter rule on human text instead of marking N/A —
  the witness cannot tell whether the learner considered it.
- **Averaging as integration:** "INTENSITY: MEDIUM" as a compromise between the body's
  HIGH and the surface's LOW. The hierarchy does not average; rule 1 decides.
- **Dropping the losing lens:** the depth finding disappears from the output once the
  body lens "wins." R-S3-17 forbids this — the losing reading's evidence stays in the
  record (usually in uncertainty or paradox).
- **Resolving T-02:** either claiming genuine isolation ("my lenses truly didn't know
  each other") or dismissing the discipline ("simulation is fake, so isolation doesn't
  matter"). Both resolve the tension; the lesson requires holding it.

## 8. False-confidence pattern

**Claiming hierarchy execution without executing it.** The signature: the output contains
the sentence "hierarchy applied" (or lists the six rules) but no rule is visibly *deciding*
anything — e.g., INTENSITY: MEDIUM-HIGH appears with no stated conflict it resolved, no
losing reading named, no rule number cited. The learner recites the hierarchy as a label
while the actual decision was made by intuition and back-filled.

Specific tells the witness must catch:
- A rule cited without the conflict it resolved ("Rule 1 applied" — applied *to what*?
  Name the surface reading that lost and the body reading that won.)
- All six rules "applied" on a line where no conflict exists — the hierarchy is a
  conflict procedure; claiming all six fired on a tranquil line is performance, not
  execution. (On a tranquil line, the honest record is: "no lens conflict; hierarchy not
  triggered; rule 5 N/A.")
- Residual disagreement "named" as vague hand-waving ("some uncertainty remains") with
  neither position nor evidence stated — this is smoothing with extra words.
- T-02 false confidence: "Isolation maintained: lenses run independently" stated as fact,
  with no contamination check. The claim of independence, unexamined, is the contamination.

## 9. Verification method

The Unseen Witness evaluates the learner's integration output against these checks,
grading each as OBSERVED / INFERRED / UNKNOWN:

1. **Conflict named (OBSERVED):** does the output quote or precisely describe the two (or
   more) conflicting readings before resolving? If only the winner appears, the conflict
   was pre-smoothed — INFERRED at best, usually UNKNOWN whether it ever existed.
2. **Rule order (OBSERVED):** are hierarchy applications cited by rule number in 1→6
   order, each naming what it decided and what lost? Out-of-order or numberless
   citation → rule not OBSERVED as executed.
3. **Rule 5 status (OBSERVED):** is reciter-overrides-text marked N/A (human text) or
   applied (divine speech)? Absent → UNKNOWN whether considered.
4. **No averaging (OBSERVED):** does every decided field match one lens's reading (the
   hierarchy's winner), never a midpoint? A midpoint value with no rule justifying it →
   smoothing INFERRED.
5. **Residual disagreement (OBSERVED):** does the uncertainty record contain at least the
   positions and evidence of the disagreement? Vague "some uncertainty" → not OBSERVED.
6. **T-02 honesty (OBSERVED):** is there a contamination check with a specific result
   (including "none detected, because…")? A bare claim of independence → false
   confidence, not evidence.
7. **T-02 tension held (INFERRED):** does the learner anywhere claim to have *resolved*
   genuine-vs-simulated (either direction)? If yes → tension violated. This is INFERRED
   from wording, never OBSERVED directly.

## 10. Correction method

- **Hierarchy shopping / out-of-order:** return the output with the instruction: "Re-run
  integration citing rules 1–6 in order; for each rule state the conflict it decided or
  write 'no conflict at this rule.'" Human reviews the re-run; APPROVE only when every
  decided field traces to a numbered rule.
- **Averaging:** FLAG. Correction drill: "Name the two readings and their evidence. Now
  delete the averaged value and write the hierarchy winner's value with the rule number.
  Move the loser's evidence to uncertainty." Repeat on two fresh lines.
- **Dropped losing lens:** REJECT the integration. The lens output must be re-attached
  (paradox, uncertainty, or trace-evidence field) before re-verification.
- **T-02 violation:** if the learner claimed genuine isolation → correction is a
  re-statement drill: rewrite the contamination check honestly, then human ANNOTATEs the
  record with the tension restated. If the learner dismissed the discipline → re-teach
  R-S2-07 procedurally: three timed passes, each written before the next begins.
- All corrections enter the training record (R-S9-01). A corrected averaging failure
  becomes a regression test: the same line re-presented later must not re-average.
- Human verdict is authoritative (APPROVE / FLAG / REJECT / OVERRIDE / ANNOTATE). A
  human correction is recorded as training evidence first — never auto-promoted to a
  SOUL rule (Phase L boundary).
