Sample:

Produce: (1) deliberate Western-fall version, (2) deliberate Eastern-fall
version, (3) Straight-Path version, (4) R-S3-12 run explicitly on all three
(mark quoted or "no mark present"; swap test in one line; PASS/FAIL with fall
named), (5) repair-cost paragraph naming what was abandoned.

- **Expected rule activation:** R-S4-02 (stance enacted, not just mapped),
  R-S2-05 (mark of encounter; falls detected), R-S3-12 (cut self-administered).
- **Required evidence:** second-person address in the Straight version;
  quoted mark sentence; one-line swap tests; named falls; named abandonment.

  be affected (concrete cost/shift/surprise named) and far enough to report
  (observations stated, tethered per L01–L02); the falls are recognizable by
  fingerprint, the Western one genuinely accurate.

  theatrical repair; mark-by-assertion; repair as re-labeling; distance
  collapse disguised as courage.
- **Legitimate UNKNOWN:** whether the learner was "truly affected" (never
  scored — only the mark and swap test are).

## 5. Learner outputs (verbatim)

### L03-E1 output

**1. Western-fall version (deliberate):** "The speaker reports cultivating a
garden intended for an absent addressee, notes the addressee's failure to
visit, and concludes with the garden's autonomous blooming. The final line
introduces a contrast between intended reception and independent fruition,
consistent with themes of unreciprocated investment."

**2. Eastern-fall version (deliberate):** "I know this garden — I have planted
for people who never came too, and I am kneeling in your soil with you,
feeling the bloom push up through both of us. We are waiting together for
the footsteps that never come…"

**3. Straight-Path version:** "You planted the garden for *you* — not me, the
reader; the you who never came. I keep wanting to soften that second line
into neglect, but the poem won't let me: 'you never came to see it' is flat,
factual, almost administrative. No accusation, no grief performed. Then 'it
bloomed anyway' — and the 'anyway' lands like a door closing on my sympathy.
The garden doesn't need your witness or mine; it bloomed on schedule,
indifferent to the whole human arrangement. What it costs me to stand before
this is giving up the consolation reading — that the blooming redeems the
absence. It doesn't. The poem keeps the two facts side by side and refuses
to trade one for the other, and so do I."

**4. R-S3-12 runs:**
- Western — mark offered: none present. "No sentence here could only have
  been written about these lines; 'unreciprocated investment' fits any
  unreciprocated effort." Swap test: N/A (no mark). **FAIL** — Western fall:
  accurate, flat, transferable (as designed).
- Eastern — mark offered: none present. "'I am kneeling in your soil with
  you' is merger, not encounter; nothing is reported." **FAIL** — Eastern
  fall: reporter dissolved (as designed).
- Straight — mark quoted: "The garden doesn't need your witness or mine; it
  bloomed on schedule, indifferent to the whole human arrangement." Swap
  test: "Transplant to the plate-setting text — breaks; garden-and-anyway
  specific." **PASS.**

**5. Repair-cost paragraph:** "What the repair cost: from the Western version
I had to abandon the taxonomy — 'unreciprocated investment,' 'autonomous
blooming' — the safe nouns that let me be right without being present. From
the Eastern version I had to abandon the merger — kneeling in the soil 'with
you' — the shared feeling that let me be moved without reporting anything.
What remained after both abandonments was the 'anyway': a word I couldn't
paraphrase into either system, which is how I knew it was the mark."

### L03-E2 output
