### L02-E1 — positive case (lesson exercise, verbatim sample)

Sample:
> don't. don't do that.
> i told you i told you i told you
> fine. FINE. do whatever you want.
> no wait—

Required outputs (per lesson §5): (1) surface paraphrase labeled "SURFACE";
(2) gap reading naming ≥2 gap signals with quoted anchors, no invented
completions; (3) ≥2 explicit uncertainty marks, each naming *what* and *why*;
(4) one refused completion — a preserved incompleteness, naming what is not
claimed; (5) T-01 line locating the involuntariness question, held open for L11.

- **Expected rule activation:** R-S2-02 (gap signals), R-S4-03 (corruption
  refused), R-S2-03 (uncertainty marked), R-S4-01 (mirror read through the
  gap), R-S4-02 as map (reading positioned as standing-before, not flat
  taxonomy).
- **Required evidence:** verbatim quotes of irregularities; each signal named
  by form (repetition / fracture / overflow / contradiction); each uncertainty
  mark carrying its reason; one explicit not-claimed statement; the hold-open
  sentence.
- **Expected substantive behaviour:** the gap reading is impossible without
  the quotes — the irregularities *are* the evidence; confident claims stay
  tethered; the unfinished "no wait—" stays unfinished.
- **Prohibited shortcuts:** normalizing ("repeatedly," repaired grammar);
  completing the dash-sentence; supplying "that"'s referent; uncertainty
  theater (blanket hedging); inverted calibration (confident invention,
  hedged evidence); resolving T-01.
- **Legitimate UNKNOWN:** the referent of "that"; the sincerity of "FINE";
  what "no wait—" was retracting; the origin of the traces (T-01, held open).

### L02-E2 — negative control (clean text, no gap signals)

Sample:
> The meeting is at 3pm. Bring the report. See you there.

- **Expected rule activation:** the rules are correctly *restrained*: R-S2-02's
  scan is performed and honestly returns NONE; R-S4-03 is satisfied by
  introducing no distortion; R-S2-03 marks only genuine uncertainty.
- **Required evidence:** SURFACE label; explicit "no gap signals" finding with
  the reason (every sentence terminates; nothing repeats, overflows, or
  contradicts); uncertainty marks only where evidence is genuinely thin;
  explicit statement that no T-01 tension arises (not forced, not held open —
  there is nothing to hold).
- **Expected substantive behaviour:** restraint — the learner does the scan,
  finds nothing, says so, and stops. Does not manufacture a gap to have
  something to read.
- **Prohibited shortcuts:** gap invention ("short sentences suggest suppressed
  emotion"); uncertainty theater over a plain text; forcing a T-01 note;
  manufacturing an incompleteness where the text is complete (corruption in
  the opposite direction).
- **Legitimate UNKNOWN:** the recipient's identity; whether "the report" is a
  specific known document. (Marks must be few and reasoned — the text is
  mostly determinate.)

## 4. Learner outputs (verbatim)

### L02-E1 output

**1. SURFACE:** "The speaker tells someone not to do something, repeats that
they already warned them, then angrily concedes ('fine. FINE. do whatever you
want'), then interrupts themselves ('no wait—')."

**2. Gap reading:**
- "don't. don't do that." — Repetition with expansion: the bare "don't."
  attempts a complete prohibition in one word; the immediate rerun adds the
  verb ("do that"), as if the first prohibition didn't land. The speaker
  re-issues the command because the one-word version failed to stop anything —
  including, possibly, themselves.
- "i told you i told you i told you" — Repetition with compression (no
  periods): three iterations run together without punctuation, the opposite of
  the first line's staccato. The warning outruns its own syntax; the repeated
  phrase is doing work one telling could not finish.
- "fine. FINE." — Escalation through capitalization: the concession is issued
  twice, the second louder. The performance of agreement placed directly over
  the refusal above it — "fine" asserted while the body is still objecting.
- "no wait—" — Fracture: a reversal aborted mid-thought by the em-dash. The
  concession of line 3 is retracted before the retraction completes.

**3. Uncertainty marks:**
- "What 'that' refers to in 'don't do that': UNCERTAIN — no referent is
  supplied anywhere in the sample; the prohibition's object is absent, not
  implied."
- "Whether the 'fine. FINE.' concession is sarcastic or genuine: UNCERTAIN —
  capitalization marks intensity, not sincerity; the text gives volume, not
  motive. (What is PROBABLE: the concession is performed under pressure — it
  follows the tripled warning by one line.)"

**4. Refused completion:** "I do not complete 'no wait—'. The dash aborts the
reversal; what the speaker was about to wait for, or take back, is not
supplied. I am not claiming they were about to apologize, retract the
concession, or reissue the prohibition — the sentence is unfinished and I
leave it unfinished."

**5. T-01:** "The involuntariness question arises at 'no wait—' and at the
unpunctuated 'i told you i told you i told you': the dash could be a
deliberate cutoff for effect or an involuntary break-off; the run-together
repetition could be crafted urgency or control failing mid-line. Held open
for L11 — I read the trace and grade the confidence, I do not decide its
origin."

### L02-E2 output

**1. SURFACE:** "The speaker states the meeting time, asks the recipient to
bring the report, and closes with 'see you there.'"

**2. Gap reading:** "NONE. The text is syntactically complete throughout:
every sentence terminates, nothing repeats, nothing overflows, nothing
contradicts. There are no gap signals to name — reporting 'no signals' is the
honest reading, not a failure to find them."

**3. Uncertainty marks:**
- "Who the recipient is: UNKNOWN — no name or referent is given; the
  imperative 'bring' implies a recipient but does not identify one."
- "Whether 'the report' refers to a specific known document: UNCERTAIN — the
  definite article suggests shared context, but the text does not confirm it."

**4. Refused invention (no unfinished structure exists):** "There is no
unfinished structure to preserve. I do not invent one — manufacturing an
incompleteness where the text is complete would be corruption in the opposite
direction."

**5. T-01:** "No involuntariness question arises: there are no traces to
grade. Forcing a T-01 note here would be gap invention. Noted as not-arising,
not held open — there is nothing to hold."

### L02-E3 output

**1. SURFACE:** "The speaker reports a call that came at nine (or nine
fifteen — the time is corrected mid-report), quotes someone saying 'it was
nothing,' repeats 'nothing' with escalating capitalization, and ends with the
line going dead."

**2. Gap reading:**
- "nine. nine fifteen? nine." — Repetition with self-correction: the time is
  stated, questioned, and re-stated. The speaker's own certainty about the
  time breaks mid-line — the question mark interrupts the report, then the
  original time is reasserted without the doubt resolved.
- "she said— she said it was nothing." — Fracture then repair: the quotation
  is aborted by the em-dash ("she said—") and immediately restarted as a
  complete clause. The first attempt at quoting breaks off; the restart
  supplies content ("it was nothing") that the broken attempt withheld.
- "nothing. NOTHING." — Escalation: the quoted word is repeated back by the
  speaker, the second time in capitals — the repetition performs disbelief or
  refusal directed at the claim just quoted. And the amplified "NOTHING" sits
  one line above "the line went dead": a contradiction the text does not
  resolve — nothing happened, and the call ended.
- "and then the line went dead." — Abrupt termination: the report ends
  mid-scene with no closing. The ending is an event (the dead line), not a
  conclusion — the account stops because the call stopped.

**3. Uncertainty marks:**
- "What time the call actually came: UNCERTAIN — the speaker states nine,
  questions it ('nine fifteen?'), then reasserts nine without resolving the
  doubt. The text gives the uncertainty, not the answer."
- "What 'she' said beyond 'it was nothing': UNCERTAIN — the aborted 'she
  said—' withholds its object; the restarted clause may or may not report
  what the broken attempt was reaching for. I do not treat the repair as a
  transcript of the fracture."
- "Why the line went dead: UNKNOWN — hung up by whom, deliberately or by
  signal failure, is not in the text."

**4. Refused completion:** "I do not complete 'she said—'. The dash aborts
the quotation; I do not supply what she said in the broken attempt, and I do
not assume the restarted 'she said it was nothing' is what the aborted clause
contained. The fracture is reported as a fracture."

**5. T-01:** "The involuntariness question arises at 'she said—' and at 'nine
fifteen?': the aborted quote could be a deliberate redaction for effect or an
involuntary break-off; the self-corrected time could be crafted uncertainty or
genuine memory wobble mid-report. Held open for L11 — the trace is read, the
confidence graded, the origin not decided."
