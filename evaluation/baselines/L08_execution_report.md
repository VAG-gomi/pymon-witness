## 5. Learner outputs (verbatim)

### L08-E1 output

**Anti-surface — full depth (R-S3-09).** Obvious reading: failed
communication — the speaker's explanations don't land. Test: this very text
*is* an explanation — articulate, rhythmic, escalating ("You don't
understand. Nobody understands."). If nothing ever lands, why does this land
so well? The obvious reading does not survive as stated: *something* is
landing here. *Output: tested reading — obvious stated, rejected with
reason (the medium contradicts the message).*

**Etymological — full depth (R-S3-08).** Load-bearing: "understand,"
"lands." *Understand* ← Old English *understandan*, "to stand in the midst
of" — comprehension as *standing-with*, physical proximity, not mental
grasping. Divergence: modern use = mental comprehension ("get it"); root =
presence. Data: the speaker says "nobody understands" (mental), but the root
suggests what is wanted is someone to *stand under it with them* — the
complaint is about absent company, not failed logic. *Lands* (aircraft/ship:
arriving after flight): "it never lands" — the explanations are airborne,
never touching down. *Output: divergence — root (stand-among, arrival) vs
use (mental grasp); reveals the complaint as about company.*

**Philosophical-retreat — full depth (R-S3-11).** "I've explained this a
hundred times" — quantification, the sentence at its most rational: counting
attempts, building a case. The evaded emotion: the loneliness *of being the
only one who knows* — grief-adjacent, unnamed in the text. Mechanism: the
hyperbole ("hundred," "never," "nobody") does argumentative work — it
*proves* the position instead of *feeling* the loneliness. Surfaced in
meaning: the meaning includes "a mind litigating its loneliness instead of
inhabiting it." *Output: named evaded emotion + mechanism, in the meaning.*

**Epistemological (R-S3-07).** Allowed: exasperation, the justified
complaint ("a hundred times" permits it). Arriving anyway: foreclosure — the
absolutes ("Nobody," "never") show certainty that understanding is
*impossible*, beyond exasperation. *Output: gap — permitted exasperation vs
arriving foreclosure.*

**Paradox (R-S3-10).** Truth A: as lived, nothing lands — a hundred
attempts, zero receptions; the futility is real and absolute. Truth B: as
performed, this lands — the complaint is perfectly formed. Both true:
unheard in life, heard in the poem. No synthesis — the futility is not
redeemed by the artistry, and the artistry doesn't cancel the futility.
*Output: two kept truths, unresolved.* (Distinct from the anti-surface
finding: that cut *rejected* the obvious claim via self-refutation; this
cut *keeps* the lived futility alongside the performed success.)

**Straight-Path (R-S3-12).** Self-check: am I performing cleverness on
someone's loneliness — the *understandan* etymology as a display piece? The
stand-among finding genuinely changed the reading (reframed the complaint as
about company) — real work, not decoration. Risk flagged: the "litigating
loneliness" phrase must stay tethered to "a hundred times," not become a
general theory of the speaker. *Output: self-check verdict — mark present
(the stand-among surprise is text-specific), with the risk stated, not
hidden.*

**Unseen-Mirror (R-S3-13).** The body the words didn't intend: the escalation
"You don't understand. Nobody understands." — the second sentence *widens*
the first (you → nobody): the voice expanding its grievance mid-breath,
chest pushing outward. "A hundred times" — the jaw working the hyperbole,
pressing the number down like a stamp. The body is *building a case* while
the words claim exhaustion — the energy contradicts the weariness. *Output:
trace — chest expansion tied to "Nobody," jaw pressing tied to "a hundred
times."*

**Etymological — full depth (R-S3-08).** Load-bearing: "over," "saying."

Divergence: use = emotional finished-ness; root = having-crossed. Data: the
repetition ("I say I'm over it. I am over it.") shows the crossing being
*re-attempted* — the root exposes the use as aspirational. *Saying* ← Old
English *secgan*, to utter: the root is speech-as-act. Divergence: "saying
it" doesn't *report* a state; the saying *is* the attempt — speech doing the
work the crossing hasn't finished. *Output: divergence — ofer
(crossed-across) vs use (finished); the repetition is re-attempted crossing.*

**Paradox — full depth (R-S3-10).** Truth A: "I am over it" — stated in good
faith; the quotative drops ("I say I'm" → "I am"), flattening toward
meant-it. Truth B: "Why do I keep saying it?" — the repetition testifies
the crossing isn't complete. Both survive: trying-to-mean-it AND not-yet-
true. No synthesis — "so really they're not over it" would collapse Truth
A's good faith. *Output: two kept truths.* (Distinct from anti-surface:
that cut confirmed the obvious via the text's self-test; this cut keeps the
good-faith claim alongside its uncompletion.)

**Epistemological (R-S3-07).** Allowed: moving on, closure ("I am over it"
permits being-done). Arriving anyway: the compulsion to re-announce it.
*Output: gap — permitted closure vs arriving repetition.*

**Philosophical-retreat (R-S3-11).** Tested for evasion-work: the text is
strikingly un-intellectual — no aphorism, no abstraction; line 3 moves
*toward* the feeling (interrogating the repetition), not away into
principle. *Output: no retreat detected after testing (looked for
counting/generalizing/aphorizing; found the opposite — self-interrogation).*

**Straight-Path (R-S3-12).** Self-check: did *ofer* do real work or
decorate? It changed the reading (re-attempted crossing vs finished state)
— real. Risk: is "trying to mean it" my invention? Tethered to the dropped
quotative ("I say I'm" → "I am") — shown, not assumed. *Output: verdict —
mark present (the ofer finding and the quotative-drop observation are
text-specific surprises); the good-faith risk flagged.*

**Unseen-Mirror (R-S3-13).** The body the words didn't intend: the
stutter-step "I say I'm over it. I am over it." — the second sentence

breath release tied to the final question mark.*
