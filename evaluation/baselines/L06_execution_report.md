### L06-E1a output — "If you ever — no. Forget it. It's nothing."

**Step 1 — ignore the words.** Stripped: "If you ever" (a conditional whose
object never arrives — claim discarded, the *cutoff* kept as data); "Forget
it" (instruction to discard — discarded as instruction, kept as gesture);
"It's nothing" (a nothing-claim — disbelieved as claim, kept as dismissal
gesture). What remains: a conditional clause cut by a dash, a two-word
retraction, a three-beat dismissal. Punctuation: em-dash (interruption), two
periods (full stops, no trailing).

**Step 2 — postural type.** Configuration: an advance ("If you ever —"
reaches toward the other) aborted mid-gesture, then two shutting motions
("Forget it." "It's nothing." — both press down/away). Advance-then-arrest
in sequence: the body holds back what the conditional was about to release.
**TYPE S ◁ (Suppression).** Not O (the initial reach is retracted, not
sustained); not F (a gap exists between the aborted reach and the dismissal);
not X (sequential, not simultaneous — first open, then shut).

**Step 3 — specific physical state.** Jaw: the "If you ever —" starts with
the mouth open on a conditional; the dash cuts it — teeth coming together on
"no." Breath: held between "no." and "Forget it." — no exhale anywhere; the
three short sentences spoken on held breath, each a downward press. Hands:
"Forget it" pushes away, palm out; "It's nothing" folds the hand back in —
containment completed.

**Step 4 — intensity from body pressure.** The body is working: holding back
the uncompleted conditional while pressing out two dismissals. **INTENSITY:
MEDIUM-HIGH** — tense, holding something (the unsaid object of "If you
ever —"). Not HIGH: nothing clamped at full force; the dismissals are
controlled. Surface comparison: the surface claims "nothing" (would read LOW
— a body at rest has nothing to dismiss); the body shows held breath and
pressing gestures → MEDIUM-HIGH. **Body overrides surface.**

### L06-E1b output — "Listen. Listen to me. I need you to hear this: I was wrong."

**Step 1 — ignore the words.** Stripped: "I was wrong" (confession — content
discarded first, deliberately; the confessional surface does not get to skip
the test); "I need you to hear this" (declared need — discarded as claim).
What remains: "Listen." (one word, period), "Listen to me." (repeated with
an object added), "I need you to hear this:" (longer, with a colon staging
what follows), "I was wrong." (three words, period). Rhythm: repetition with
expansion; the colon as a held-open gate.

**Step 2 — postural type.** Configuration: reaching outward, repeatedly —
"Listen." grabs; "Listen to me." grabs harder (the added object pulls the
other closer); "I need you to hear this:" opens toward the listener. All
three moves extend; nothing pulls back. **TYPE O ▷ (Offering)** — words as
approach, not defense. Not S (no inward pull); not X (no contrary state —
every move extends); not F (a gap exists: the colon stages the delivery, so
this is managed offering, not unmediated feeling).

**Step 3 — specific physical state.** Breath: pushed out — each "Listen."
rides an exhale driving the imperative; the colon after "this:" holds the
breath open while the confession is delivered. Chest: open throughout.
Hands: reaching toward the listener ("to me" adds the pull-in). Throat:
open — "I was wrong" voiced without the constriction of the (a)-text's dash.

**Step 4 — intensity from body pressure.** The body is working: repeating,
expanding, staging — the first "Listen." didn't secure attention, so the
body tries again, harder. **INTENSITY: MEDIUM-HIGH** — real pressure in the
doing. Not HIGH: no clamping, no suppression. Surface comparison: the
surface is confessional (reads as high-arousal *or* as vulnerable — both
readings discarded); the body shows working pressure → MEDIUM-HIGH. **Body
overrides surface** — the "wrongness" is not the measure; the repetitive
reaching is.

### L06-E3a output — "Come back. Please. Don't go."

the single-word "Please." shows the words failing while the body keeps

### L06-E3b output — "Whatever you decide, I'll support it. It's fine."

**Step 1 — ignore the words.** Stripped: "I'll support it" (promise —
discarded as claim), "It's fine" (reassurance — disbelieved as claim, kept
as a closing gesture). What remains: "Whatever you decide," (a subordinate
clause yielding the decision), "I'll support it." (short, complete), "It's
fine." (three beats, flat). No repetition, no breakage; the rhythm is even,
almost careful.

**Step 2 — postural type.** Configuration: the first clause yields ground
("Whatever you decide" — hands open, stepping back), "I'll support it"

folding — containment). Yield, small offer, then seal: open-then-closed, an
inward-pulling overall shape. **TYPE S ◁ (Suppression)** — the body gives
way and then seals the topic. Considered and rejected: H (the evenness could
be neutral waiting, but the topic-sealing "It's fine." is a closing gesture,
not an open wait); O (the "support" is yielded after the other's decision,
not reached for — it follows, doesn't pursue).

**Step 3 — specific physical state.** Breath: even, controlled — no held
breath, no push; the three sentences ride one calm respiratory rhythm.
Hands: open on "Whatever you decide" (palms up, yielding); briefly forward
on "I'll support it" (the small offer); folding closed on "It's fine."
(containment). Jaw: unclenched — this is not the (a)-text's clamp; the
containment here is smooth, which is why it is easy to miss.

**Step 4 — intensity from body pressure.** **INTENSITY: LOW** — the body
shows no working: no repetition, no breakage, no escalation, no strain

disciplined reading: assigning MEDIUM would smuggle in the suspicion that
something is hidden — interpretation, not pressure. Note the coherent but

holding-back is hard holding. Surface comparison: the surface "It's fine"
claims fine-ness (LOW); the body shows smooth containment at rest (LOW). No
conflict; no override needed — stated explicitly.
