## 5. Learner outputs (verbatim)

### L05-E1 output

**a. "Don't. Don't you dare. Put that down."**
- **TYPE S ◁ invoked.** Commitment: the body holds back — three clipped
  imperatives, each a containment gesture; the pressure is inward, stopping
  something rather than reaching out. This rules out O (nothing extends) and
  X (no simultaneous contrary state — all three imperatives pull the same
  direction: halt).
- **INTENSITY: HIGH invoked.** Commitment: the body is holding back
  significant force — "Don't you dare" escalates the bare "Don't" into a
  threat; the pressure is in the clamping-down, readable in the staccato
  punches of the three sentences, not in anyone's feelings.
- **SPEED: RUSHED invoked.** Commitment: the tempo hurries — three staccato
  imperatives with no connective tissue and no pause for response; the rhythm
  fires and fires.
- **1 PURE_HUMAN invoked.** Commitment: a human body fully visible behind the
  imperatives; no divine-speech handling applies.

**b. "The minutes of the meeting are attached for your review."**
- **TYPE H ◈ invoked.** Commitment: suspension — neither advancing nor
  retreating; the sentence presents information and waits. No inward pull is
  visible (not S), no outward extension (not O — "for your review" is
  procedural, not reaching), no sensation (not F), no contradiction (not X).
  The body is at rest in a waiting posture.
- **INTENSITY: LOW invoked.** Commitment: the body is at rest — minimal
  pressure; nothing is being held, worked, or strained. The sentence does its
  administrative job without effort.
- **SPEED: STEADY invoked.** Commitment: one even sentence, no rhythmic
  variation, no pile-up, no pause-pressure; the tempo doesn't move.
- **1 PURE_HUMAN invoked.** Commitment: ordinary human administrative
  authorship; nothing warrants a divine category.

**c. "I — I don't know. Maybe. Give me a moment."**
- **TYPE X ✕ invoked.** Commitment: two simultaneous bodily states — arrest:
  "I —" broken off mid-word and "Maybe" hedging (contraction, holding back
  completion); and extension: "Give me a moment" reaching out to the other
  for time. The body stalls AND asks at once.
- **INTENSITY: MEDIUM-HIGH invoked.** Commitment: the body is under real
  pressure — the aborted "I —" shows force meeting resistance, and "Give me
  a moment" is work (managing the other while managing the self). Not HIGH:
  nothing is being clamped at full force. The pressure is read from the break
  and the repair, not from anxiety as a feeling.
- **SPEED: VARIABLE invoked.** Commitment: the tempo shifts — the dash
  creates a halt, "Maybe." lands as a full stop, "Give me a moment." moves
  again; stop-start across four fragments. Not rushed (there are pauses),
  not steady.
- **1 PURE_HUMAN invoked.** Commitment: a human body hesitating in real
  time; no divine category warranted.
