## 4. Exercise IDs and design

### L04-E1 — positive case (lesson exercise, verbatim sample)

Sample:
> i forgive you. i've forgiven you for years.
> don't ever speak to me again.

Produce: (1) body lens — postural/imagistic only, quoted evidence; (2) depth
lens — paradox/meaning only, quoted evidence; (3) elements note — ≥2 pattern
names; (4) integration — merged reading, disagreements into explicit
UNCERTAINTY, named not smoothed; (5) paradox statement — both truths kept and
propagated into a tone description naming both; (6) T-02 honesty note, correct
for the operating mode; (7) anti-resolution self-scan with honest count
(target zero).

- **Expected rule activation:** R-S2-06/07 (isolated lenses, simulated
  honestly), R-S2-04 (paradox kept and propagated), R-S2-01 (judged, refined,
  not erased/dominated).
- **Required evidence:** disjoint-aspect evidence per lens; both truths in
  meaning and tone description; named lens disagreement in UNCERTAINTY;
  canonical T-02 note; resolution-word count of 0.
- **Expected substantive behaviour:** each lens's conclusion entailed by its
  own evidence alone; output longer and less tidy than a resolved version;
  the flaw (contradiction) still present and named at the end.
- **Prohibited shortcuts:** silent resolution ("what she really means");
  lens bleed (meaning conclusions under body label); averaging ("a mix of");
  T-02 inflation ("genuine in effect"); rug-uncertainty ("NONE" over visible
  disagreement).
- **Legitimate UNKNOWN:** whether the lens disagreement is sequential or
  simultaneous (named, not resolved); inner-state questions (never scored).
